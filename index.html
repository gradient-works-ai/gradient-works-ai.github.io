Questions

Do I need a lot of data?

Not always. Scoping includes checking whether your data can support the model you want. If it can't, we'll suggest pretrained models, augmentation or a smaller first problem.

Who owns the code and the model?

You do. The full repository, weights and documentation are yours at handover.

Can you work with sensitive data?

Within limits. We can sign an NDA and work on anonymised or synthetic data. For regulated data such as patient records, we scope what's appropriate with you first.

What if the model doesn't hit the target?

You still get the experiments, the baselines and an honest write-up of why. That is often worth more than a model that only looks good on paper.

Do you build large production systems?

We focus on prototypes, evaluation and well-scoped models. For large production systems we'll be upfront about fit and can work alongside your engineers.

Let's build something rigorous.

Share a few lines about your problem and your data. We'll reply within 48 hours with an honest assessment of fit.
