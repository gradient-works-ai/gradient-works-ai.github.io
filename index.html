FAQ

Questions

Do I need a lot of data?

Not always. Part of scoping is checking whether your data can support the model you want. If it can't, we'll say so, and suggest pretrained models, augmentation or a smaller problem.

Who owns the code and the model?

You do. You get the full repository, weights and documentation at handover.

Can you work with sensitive data?

Yes, within limits. We can sign an NDA and work on anonymised or synthetic data. For regulated data such as patient records, we'll scope what's appropriate with you first.

What if the model doesn't hit the target?

You still get the experiments, the baselines and an honest write-up of why it didn't work. That is often worth more than a model that only looks good on paper.

Do you do large production systems?

We focus on prototypes, evaluation and well-scoped models. For large production systems we'll be upfront about fit and can work alongside your engineers.
